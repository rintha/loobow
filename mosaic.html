About us

Where art meets architecture.

Founded by Anthony, Loobow Mosaic is a studio devoted to the ancient craft of mosaic, reimagined for contemporary spaces. Every piece begins as a conversation and ends as a one-of-a kind artwork hand-laid tessera by tessera in glass, marble, stone and gold-leaf smalti.

From a statement wall in a luxury residence to a hotel lobby or a quiet courtyard, our mosaics are made to be lived with for generations.

Our craft

What we create.

Bespoke Mosaic Art

One-of-a-kind artworks designed around your vision, from abstract to classical.

Byzantine-Inspired Mosaics

Gold-leaf smalti, jewel tones and timeless iconography in the Byzantine tradition – for domes, niches and arches in sacred spaces, and in homes that celebrate heritage.

Architectural Mosaics

Walls, floors, facades and pools engineered for lasting performance.

Custom Commissions

Brand marks, murals and signature pieces for hospitality and private clients.

Designer & Architect Collaboration

Drawings, samples and on-site coordination for interior and architectural projects.
