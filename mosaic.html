About us

Where art meets architecture.

Founded by Anthony, Loobow Mosaic is a studio devoted to the ancient craft of mosaic, reimagined for contemporary spaces. Every piece begins as a conversation and ends as a one-of-a-kind artwork, hand-laid tessera by tessera.

Architectural Mosaics

Byzantine-Inspired Mosaics

Materials & craft

Crafted by hand.

Natural Stone

Marble in a full range of colors, including Carrara and Calacatta, plus travertine and limestone. Hand-cut for warmth and texture.

Smalti

Traditional opaque glass with rich, saturated color.

Mother-of-Pearl

Iridescent shell for fine detail and name plaques.

Venetian Art Glass

Luminous, richly colored glass.

Murano Art Glass

Handcrafted glass with unique color and depth.

Vitreous Glass

Durable glass tile for walls, floors and pools.

Stained Glass

Translucent color that plays with light.
